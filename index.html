Our story

Ocean-view dining in Crescent City

Seaside Restaurant & Bar is inside the Oceanfront Lodge at 100 A Street, where tall windows look straight out over the Pacific. Come in for breakfast, stop by for a bowl of chowder at lunch, or watch the sun go down over the ocean at dinner.

Our clam chowder is made fresh every day with clams, smoky bacon, potatoes and vegetables in a creamy broth. Have it by the bowl, in our homemade bread bowl, or with a side of coconut prawns.

We're about 500 yards from Battery Point Lighthouse, an easy walk from our door.

Full Bar

Cocktails, beer and wine with an ocean view.

Every day

Breakfast, lunch and dinner

Mornings start at 7 with omelets, breakfast burritos, biscuits and gravy, and our Seaside Breakfast Bowl. The clam chowder is on the menu all day.

For lunch and dinner there's seafood, pasta, burgers, tacos and stir-fries, with a kids menu and desserts too. Fettuccine Alfredo is our most popular dish.

Order online for pickup, get delivery through DoorDash, or call us at (707) 465-5400.

FAQ

Questions

Where is Seaside Restaurant & Bar?

Inside the Oceanfront Lodge at 100 A Street, Crescent City, CA 95531, about 500 yards from Battery Point Lighthouse.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes, every day from 7:00 to 10:00 AM.

What are your lunch and dinner hours?

Monday to Thursday 11:30 AM to 8:30 PM, and Friday to Sunday 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Call us at (707) 465-5400 to reserve a table.

Is your clam chowder homemade?

Yes. We make it fresh every day. At breakfast it comes in our homemade bread bowl; at lunch and dinner, have it by the bowl or in the bread bowl.

Does the restaurant have ocean views?

Yes. The dining room and the bar look out over the Pacific Ocean.

Can I order takeout or delivery?

Yes. Order online for pickup, or get delivery through DoorDash.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes. There's a kids menu at lunch and dinner, and kids pancakes at breakfast.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are welcome in our outdoor seating area.

Is there a bar?

Yes, a full bar with cocktails, beer and wine.
